Token Launch Radar

Every genuine launch candidate in the snapshot — a protocol that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that already trades — ranked by what its token would be worth if it launched one today.

How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × benchmark × growth × age. The benchmark is the median market-cap / TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category (the cross-category median when a category has fewer than five of them). Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped and damped. Age is a saturating bonus for how long the protocol has run with no token. Nothing here names a protocol in advance: every figure is computed from the snapshot.

A protocol is listed as pre-token when DefiLlama records no token for it. That is a record, not a proof: a listed protocol may still have a token its record does not carry. The board ranks genuine launch candidates only: wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left out, however large their TVL — WBTC is Bitcoin on Ethereum, not a protocol waiting on a launch. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: the board ranks all launch candidates, not only the forty on it.
